Rainbow Bottles

Privacy Policy

A clear summary of how the game handles information.

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Information in the game

Rainbow Bottles does not require an account. Game progress and sound preferences are stored on your device. The game does not send this gameplay information to Sinan Ulusoy or Wibbi Wobbo.

The game does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Purchases

The optional one-time Unlock All Levels purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles payment information; Sinan Ulusoy does not receive your payment card details. Apple may process information about your Apple Account and purchase under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the details you include are used to respond to your request and help troubleshoot the issue. Please do not include payment card details in a support message.

Retention and deletion

Sinan Ulusoy does not receive or retain game progress or sound preferences, so there is no developer-held gameplay data to delete. To remove locally stored game data, delete Rainbow Bottles from your device. Device backups are managed through your Apple settings.

Support messages are retained only as long as needed to respond to and resolve the request. You can ask for a support message to be deleted by emailing the address below. The game does not request tracking consent, so there is no in-app tracking consent to withdraw.
