Privacy Policy

A clear summary of how the game handles information.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Information in the game

Cozy Threads does not require a player account or ask players to submit personal information to use the game. The game stores puzzle progress and preferences on your device.

Gameplay information is stored locally and is not sent to Sinan Ulusoy or Wibbi Wobbo. The game does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Purchases

An optional one-time purchase unlocks levels 11–100. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The game uses the resulting entitlement to provide purchased content. Apple handles payment information; Sinan Ulusoy does not receive payment card details. Apple may process Apple Account and transaction information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the details you choose to include are used to answer your request and help troubleshoot the issue. Please do not send account passwords or payment card details.

Retention and deletion

Sinan Ulusoy does not receive or retain game progress, artwork, or preferences. Removing the app removes its locally stored data from that installation. Device backup handling is controlled by Apple. Apple may retain transaction records under its own policies.

Support messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to and resolve the request. You can ask for a support message to be deleted by emailing the address below. The game does not request tracking consent, so there is no in-game tracking consent to withdraw.
