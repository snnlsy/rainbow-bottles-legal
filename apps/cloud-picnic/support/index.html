Purchase or restore help

Picnics 1–10 are free. The optional one-time purchase unlocks Picnics 11–100. To restore a previous purchase, open the in-game purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Apple handles billing and refunds. For billing questions or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
