Cloud Picnic

Privacy Policy

A clear summary of how the game handles information.

Effective date: November 25, 2026

Information in the game

Cloud Picnic does not require a player account and does not ask players to provide personal information. Game progress, the current puzzle state, sound preferences, and a parent-selected local access setting are stored on your device so the game can resume where you left off.

This information is not sent to Sinan Ulusoy or Wibbi Wobbo. Cloud Picnic does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking.

Purchases

The optional one-time Unlock All Levels purchase is processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. The game receives the purchase entitlement status needed to unlock levels and restore a prior purchase. Apple handles payment information; Sinan Ulusoy does not receive payment card details. Apple may process information about your Apple Account and purchase under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, your email address and the details you choose to include are used to answer your request and help troubleshoot the issue. Please do not send account passwords or payment card details.

Retention and deletion

Sinan Ulusoy does not receive or retain game progress or preferences, so there is no developer-held gameplay data to delete. Delete Cloud Picnic from your device to remove its locally stored app data. Apple may retain purchase records under its own policies.

Support messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to and resolve the request. You can ask for a support message to be deleted by emailing the address below. The game does not request tracking consent, so there is no in-game tracking consent to withdraw.
