Purchase or restore help

An optional one-time purchase unlocks all 100 garden levels. To restore a previous purchase, open the in-game grown-ups purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Apple handles billing and refunds. For billing questions or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
