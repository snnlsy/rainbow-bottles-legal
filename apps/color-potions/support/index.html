Purchase or restore help

Rainbow Bottles includes an optional one-time purchase to unlock all 100 levels. To restore a previous purchase, use Restore Purchases in the game. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Apple handles billing and refunds. For billing questions or refund requests, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
